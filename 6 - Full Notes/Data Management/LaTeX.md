# LaTeX

#### Definitions:
- TeX: A markup language, invented by Donald Knuth in 1978
- LaTeX: An extension of TeX to make it easier to use 

#### Uses:
- Useful for writing equations
- Large documents are easier to use
- Unlike Word, does not struggle to compile the table of contents of your large reports
- Automates the placement of figures and tables guided by your preferences
- Automates handling references for you
- Various reference styles available
- Very easy to use on [[UNIX]] or [[Linux]] boxes
