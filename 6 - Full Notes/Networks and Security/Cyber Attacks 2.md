# Cyber Attacks 2


#### Data Breaches:
- Yahoo had a big one in 2016 
- Up to 3 billion accounts compromised
- Stolen data:
	- Names, emails, number
	- Security questions
	- DOB
	- Hashed passwords

#### Recent Trends:
- Mostly external actors
- Financial motives
- Mostly organised crime
- Use of stolen creds

#### What happens to stolen data?
- Public disclosure to fuck people over
- Intelligence for the state
- Sold on black market

#### Distributed Denial of Service:
- Denial-of-Service (DoS) Cyber attacks:
	- Aim at making a service unavailable to users
	- Mostly done by overloading its resources
	- Commonly by service request flooding
	- When flooding of traffic is done by many sources it is Distributed DoS or DDoS

#### Botnets in DDoSes:
- Large groups of computers networked together using combined computer power to DDoS
- Usually built from vulnerable systems with no concern for who owners are
- A botnet is usually controlled by a Command&Control (C&C) infrastructure
- Trend: use IoT to form botnets

#### Why IoT devices?
- Security is often not a priority for device manufacturer
	- Poor practice, use of default passwords, open ports
	- No updates to fix vulnerabilities
	- Forgotten about by owners after installed

#### Influence Campaigns:
- Series of cyber-attacks and releases information aimed to influence thinking and choices of a large number of persons
- Use massive amounts of bots in social media platforms
	- Each bot is a surrogate account taking automatic actions to spread specific ideas/messages on social media
	- Difficult to detect and recognise as fake
		- Now even more using generative AI technologies
	- Can easily reach millions of people
	- Allegedly happened in 2016 RussiaGate 

#### Web Defaces 
- Change the appearance of a website
- Mostly by Hacktivists
	- Use known/unsophisticated vulnerabilities/techniques
- Targets chosen based on:
	- Ease to hack
	- Expected media attention
- Still a widespread phenomenon

#### Supply chain attacks:
- In a supply chain attack, the adversary compromises the weakest link in the supply chain and reach the target from there
