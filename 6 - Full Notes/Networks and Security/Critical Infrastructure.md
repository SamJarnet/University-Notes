# Critical Infrastructure

#### UK Critical National Infrastructure (CNI):
- National Infrastructures are those facilities, systems, sites, information, people networks and processes necessary for a country to function and upon which daily life depends
- Loss or compromise of the infrastructure could impact many essential services and cause significant loss of life or casualties and significant economic or social impacts
- UK National Infrastructure Sectors:
	- Chemicals, Civil Nuclear, Communication, Defence, Emergency Services, Energy, Finance, Food, Government, Health, Space, Transport and Water
- Others that require a greater level of support:
	- Data holders, digital economy touchstones, cyber security organisations, high targets

#### CIs and Industrial Control Systems (ICSs):
- Many CIs are controlled and monitored by ICS
	- Electricity generation plants
	- Transportation systems
	- Manufacturing facilities
- An ICS is made up of:
	- Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA) systems
	- Remote Terminal Units (RTUs)
	- Master Terminal Units (MTUs)
	- Programmable Logical Controllers (PLCs)
	- Human-Machine Interfaces (HMIs)
	- Intelligent Electronic Device (IED)
- The terms SCADA and ICS are often used interchangeably

- Legacy ICS components not designed with security in mind
- Security through obscurity:
	- They use proprietary and not well-known software, interfaces and protocols
	- Hence an attacker should:
		- Gather knowledge on system design
		- Access the system and learn how it works
	- Not really secure:
		- Vendors publish manuals online
		- IP leak
		- Some devices can be bought cheaply
- Air-gap ICS network from IT network
	- Makes it harder for the adversary to reach the ICS
	- Not 100% secure though
