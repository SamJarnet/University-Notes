#### Potential Dividers:
Potential dividers are resistors that direct voltage in different directions. We need these to work with the sensors, as their resistance drops the voltage goes up and vice versa. Resistors can also be switched with a LDR.

#### Analogue to Digital inputs:
- Converts analogue signals to numbers.
- Can capture signals such as audio etc.
- Can be done with an [[ADC chip]]
