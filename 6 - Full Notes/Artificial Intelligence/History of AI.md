# History of AI

#### The Birth of AI
- Focus:
	- Logic and symbolic reasoning 
- Key event:
	- 1956 Dartmouth Workshop 
- Philosophy:
	- If we can describe human intelligence in enough detail, a machine can simulate it

#### The AI Winter
- Cause:
	- Fundamental limitations in representations and techniques 
- Takeaway:
	- Early methods couldn't scale to real-world complexity; government funding was slashed following critical reports 
#### The AI Revival & Connectionism
- Focus:
	- Neural networks (Connectionism) and "Expert Systems".
- Key Concept:
	- Minsky's Society of Mind - the idea that intelligence emerges from many non-intelligent agents working together

#### The Modern Era
- Drivers:
	- Massive raw power (compute) and massive raw data
- Milestones:
	- IBM Watson (NLP), Deep Learning, and LLMs

#### Symbolic AI vs Connectionism 
- Symbolic AI (GOFAI)
	- Top-Down: Rule-based logic and symbols 
	- "If-Then" statements and search trees
	- Explainable; good for math/logic
	- Fragile; struggles with "fuzzy" real-world data
- Connectionism (Neural Networks)
	- Bottom-up: Inspired by biological neurons 
	- Learning patterns from data through weights
	- Good for perception (vision, audio, text)
	- "Blackbox" requires massive data/compute 

#### Key Concepts
- Generalisation: 
	- The ability of a model to perform well on data it has never seen before (not just memorising the training set) 
- Emergent behaviour:
	- When a complex system (like an LLM or a "Society of Mind") exhibits properties that its individual parts do not have 
- Representation: How the "world" is modelled inside the AI (e.g. as a vector in a space or a node in a logic tree)

#### Why AI is advancing now
- Breakthroughs aren't necessarily due to "new" maths (many neural net concepts are decades old) but rather:
	- Massive compute:
		- Hardware (GPUs/TPUs) capable of quadrillions of operations
	- Raw data: 
		- The internet has so much data (Corpus Linguistics)
	- Refined Algorithms: 
		- Improved versions of older ideas (like Transformers/Attention)
