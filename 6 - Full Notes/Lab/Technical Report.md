# Technical Report

#### Equality Diversity and Inclusion:

- Equality Diversity and Inclusion Industrial tribunals, newspaper headlines and online discussions often focus on issues relating to problems which arise in the workplace – covering a broad scope from gender and racial equality, disability and employment rights, general conditions of employment and health and safety. Technology companies need to understand and to address diverse needs in the goods and services which they produce. It is important therefore that employers and employees alike have a clear understanding of rights and responsibilities under employment law and the broader legal and ethical issues in the workplace, which can range from health and safety to equality. Prepare and produce a brief technical report for the Head of ECS, which: (1) Provides an introduction to and overview of the relevant legal perspectives with regard to workplace equality, diversity and inclusivity referring to appropriate legislation, (2) Explains the range of business advantages for a workplace which is aware of- and caters for -- these factors backed up by appropriate evidence, and (3) Highlights the possible implications for ECS if it does not have appropriate measures in place to deal with workplace discrimination, bullying or workers’ rights.
