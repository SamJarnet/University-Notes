# Eye and Human Vision

#### Modelling the eye in three parts
- Physical model (the eye) ->
- Experimental model (the signals) -> 
- Psychological model (the brain)

#### Human Eye
- Evolved for survival
- Function of the eye is to form an in image on the retina (on fovea)
- The lens is shaped, rather than moved
- Image is transmitted via the optic nerve
- Brain must invert the image

#### Sensors
- There must be a lot 
- Cones (10^7) and rods (10^8)
- Cones - colourl rods - greylevel
	- photopic, scotopic
- Cones come in three waves
	- 1. S - Short wavelength (blue)
	- 2. M - Medium wavelength (green)
	- 3. L - Long wavelength (red)
- Insufficient bandwidth of optic nerve implies coding
